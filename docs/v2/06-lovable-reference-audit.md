# Lovable reference audit

## Evidence status

The live URL `https://tidal-glass-showreel.lovable.app/` returned “No published build” on 2026-07-19. A 2560×6753 full-page screenshot and the handoff were subsequently supplied and preserved under `docs/v2/references/`. This audit now uses the screenshot for compositional evidence while treating its imagery, names, metrics, email, and links as non-authoritative placeholders.

The prototype remains a visual reference only. Its codebase must not be migrated into or substituted for this repository.

## Adopt

- Modern white editorial canvas with foam-white and ice-blue section atmosphere
- Ocean-ink typography and a restrained ocean-inspired blue scale
- Light-first pacing with a gradual transition into deep ocean blue
- Large video-led Selected Work compositions and asymmetrical editorial rhythm
- Capabilities as a precise numbered/list format rather than a bento card grid
- Deep-ocean About section as the primary contrast field
- Experiments/NoteZ treated as secondary to editing work
- Spacious, direct contact composition
- Selective frosted glass only where it expresses media depth or sequence
- Fine grain and extremely subtle abstract caustic light
- Small monospaced production metadata
- Generous overall section spacing calibrated for normal monitor viewports
- A quiet utility/header rail and restrained inline production metadata
- A hero with one dominant landscape frame plus smaller portrait/detail companions, provided V2 unifies them more strongly
- Large flagship work pairing media with concise project evidence rather than enclosing everything in cards
- A spacious capability ledger with fine horizontal rules, numbering, and short supporting descriptions
- Grouping About and secondary Experiments into one deep-blue chapter before returning to the light contact close
- Oversized, editorial contact typography with email-first action hierarchy

## Reject or replace

- Placeholder stock imagery and any stock media in the final portfolio
- Generic AI-generated editor portrait
- Invented statistics, client counts, outcomes, testimonials, or badges
- Placeholder email addresses or social links
- Generic project names and repeated generic descriptions
- The current Lovable hero media arrangement
- Any normal text-left/cards-right hero, unrelated floating cards, random collage, software UI, or single embedded player
- Purple-heavy AI gradients, neon cyan, literal ocean objects, bubbles, fish, coral, shells
- Generic SaaS, bento-heavy, Apple-like, or Premiere-interface imitation
- Excess glass cards or disconnected floating panels
- Any prototype behavior that removes V1 localization, fallbacks, accessibility, preview exclusivity, SEO, security, or motion policy
- The reference's editor portrait, keyboard/dancer imagery, automotive stock frame, and decorative caustic tile
- Invented `6+`, `240+`, `42 min` statistics and any unverified availability/turnaround statements
- Placeholder `hello@nosther.co`; approved contact remains `contact@nosther.site`
- Generic entries such as “NOSTHER — Season Opening,” “Coast Run,” and “Studio Notes” unless replaced by real named work
- Copying the exact extremely tall artboard spacing without viewport-specific composition

## Hero decision

The Lovable hero is not approved. The approved future concept is `Cinematic Sequence Stage`: one dominant wide showreel frame, one portrait cutaway, and one detail/motion frame connected as a unified sequence with restrained timeline/playhead/timecode cues. Real portfolio media and a strong static composition are prerequisites. Phase 3 begins with static layout only; GSAP follows approval.

The screenshot strengthens the case for overlapping scale tiers and a sequence/timeline rail, but its three frames read too much like separate floating cards and lean almost entirely automotive. V2 must use real media, communicate broader capability, and create a clearer visual/semantic sequence between frames.

## Follow-up required

No additional Lovable access is required for Phase 1. Phase 2 calibration should compare against the preserved screenshot, and Phase 3 approval must be based on real portfolio media rather than fidelity to placeholder content.
